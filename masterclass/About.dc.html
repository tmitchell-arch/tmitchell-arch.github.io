ABOUT US

Building wealth, one moment at a time

A free financial and entrepreneurship program from the Douglas County Board of Commissioners.

The Master Class Series was created to give every resident — from teens learning the basics to adults rebuilding after a setback — practical, judgment-free money education. Its 60 Moments of Truth are short vignettes that cover the decisions that shape a household's financial life.

The series moves from literacy to legacy: maintaining a financial foundation, growing investments, diversifying a portfolio, protecting what you have and transferring it to the next generation. Every vignette is free to watch, on any device.
