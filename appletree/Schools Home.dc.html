Four things that make us unique

  1. How we rank

    • Our schools are consistently rated Strong and Exceptional, meeting the highest performance standards.
    • We are the largest public preschool program in DC, serving the community since 2005.

  2. How we learn

    • Every Chance and Every Opportunity to make Every Child Ready for a successful academic life.
    • Children learn through exploration and purposeful play.
    • Every Child Ready focuses on social-emotional learning, language, literacy and STEM.

  3. Where we are

    • Campuses are a metro ride from most homes and businesses in the DC area.
    • We are in the NE, NW, SW and SE quadrants, in Wards 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8.

  4. What we do

    • A nationally acclaimed curriculum designed only for three- and four-year-olds.
    • Extensive research on how three- and four-year-olds learn makes us a leading expert in early learning.
    • Small classes, so your child gets the attention they deserve.
