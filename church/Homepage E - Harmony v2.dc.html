Plan of salvation

Believe in your heart.
Confess with your mouth.

God's plan of salvation is simple, and it's laid out in His Word. Believe in your heart that Jesus Christ died for your sins, and confess with your mouth that you accept Him as your personal Lord and Savior.

Then get to know your heavenly Father. Pray (it's just talking with God) for 5 or 10 minutes a day. Set a time each day to read your Bible, starting with the New Testament. For some that's one verse; for others, one chapter.
