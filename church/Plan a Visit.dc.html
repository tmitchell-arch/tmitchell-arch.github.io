Your first Sunday

Four things people ask before they come.

01

The worship

Worship starts at 11:00 a.m. with Spirit-filled singing led by our Praise Team. Then the message: simple, scriptural, Christ-centered Bible teaching.

02

Children's Church

Kids under 9 have their own Bible teaching during the 11:00 a.m. service. It's engaging, age-appropriate and Christ-centered.

03

What to wear

Whatever you're comfortable in. Our church is for everyone, so you'll see casual clothes and suits worshiping side by side. From the Heart is a no-judgment zone.

04

Parking

Our lot has plenty of parking, and there is designated handicapped parking near the entrance.

During the week

There's more than Sunday.

Come back Wednesday, or take the message home with you.

Wednesdays, 7:00 p.m.

Bible Study

Sound Biblical learning that builds on your knowledge of God and of Jesus Christ our Lord.

Wednesdays, 12:00 p.m.

Noon Day Prayer

We come together at noon to make our requests known unto God. We pray ourselves happy while we intercede for one another.

Bookstore

Teachings on CD

Want to hear a message again? Sunday and Wednesday teachings are on CD at the Bookstore, so you can keep studying at home.
