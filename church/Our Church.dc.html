Our church

A family church where love abounds.

We're a non-denominational church at 2211 North Harvard Ave. Seventeen people joined on our first Sunday in 2002, and the doors have been open ever since.

Who we are

One church in Tulsa, part of a worldwide ministry.

From the Heart Church Ministries of Tulsa is part of From the Heart Church Ministries Worldwide™, a ministry given the mandate to be at the forefront of the reformation of the Church.

Dr. John A. Cherry, the Founding Episcopal Pastor of From the Heart Church Ministries, Inc.®, established our church on July 14, 2002. We're non-denominational, and our motto says what we're about: A Family Church Where Love Abounds.
