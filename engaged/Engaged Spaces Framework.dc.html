engaging
equitably.

Welcome to the heart of our mission. We initiate vibrant dialogues, provide insightful consultancies, and facilitate transformative coaching to cultivate spaces that resonate with liberty and deep community impact.

Not just a set of principles—a beacon that guides every interaction and strategy we apply.

Our theory

Individuals and collectives are continuously evolving along a spectrum of anti-racism awareness, bias comprehension, and competency.

Awareness Comprehension Competency →
From closed corridors of old power structuresto open arenas where justice and humanity converge

At the core of everything we facilitate, anti-racism, equity, and emotional intelligence serve as critical touchstones—ensuring each venture is imbued with deliberate and compassionate humanity: recognition of our individual and collective humanhood.

To authentically engage with equity as our compass, we lean into a deep-seated appreciation for the full tapestry of humanhood—disentangling from archaic narratives to dismantle the war on humanity declared by white supremacy culture, using our potent collective potential.

The framework

three pillars.

Three movements — from humanhood, through forward progress, to systemic change.

01Humanhood
Collective PotentialPower the disruption of White supremacy culture using the potential energy generated by the unique identities within your collective.
Lines of DifferenceAppreciate lines of difference — unshared identity markers and lived experiences — by recognizing them and their potential.
Lines of SimilarityAppreciate lines of similarity — shared identity markers and lived experiences — by recognizing them and their potential.
02Forward Progress
Own Your LearningOwn your responsibility to understand the impact of oppression by deepening your understanding of systemic injustice and marking pathways to freedom.
Put Off the Oppressor’s GazeRecognize and renounce beliefs and behaviors that are byproducts of White supremacy culture; develop a liberatory mindset.
03Fuel Systemic Change
Assess + AcknowledgeInspect your systems and their origins. Reckon with their conception and current (and former) operating ecosystems.
Design + DistributeDesign reformed systems anchored in DEIJB that are equipped to maintain equitable engagement even amidst systemic barriers.
All three lead tothe goal.

Disrupt oppressive culture and engage freely in equitable practices that produce equitable outcomes toward the liberation of all humankind.

In practice

what each pillar asks of us.

01

Humanhood

is an invitation to…
Acknowledging our shared humanityFinding unity in our similarities and strength in our diversities.
Mobilizing collective potencyChanneling the unique energies inside our community to upheave white supremacy culture.
Advancing education and leadershipFostering a lifetime commitment to personal growth and societal change.
Cultivating enduring systemic reformPlanting seeds of equity that grow into structures capable of sheltering justice and inclusiveness.
02

Forward Progress

The courageous commitment to self-directed learning—not superficially, but as a profound and personal understanding of systemic injustice and the active pursuit of genuine freedom. It’s about dethroning the oppressor’s gaze and adopting a mindset of liberation.

03

Fueling Systemic Change

First we acknowledge and confront the roots of our existing systems—thorough introspection, challenging the underpinnings of our professional practices, and reimagining them. Then we design and proliferate systems infused with diversity, equity, inclusion, justice, and belonging (DEIJB), resilient against systemic barriers to maintain equitable engagement.

Our ethos, translated into practices that yield spaces with outcomes as equitable as the intentions behind them.

We don’t just envision educational and professional environments that reflect the kaleidoscope of identities within them—we take concrete action to build them. Connect with us, and together we can actualize equitable outcomes that revel in our shared humanhood.
