65 pieces across four drops for an apparel brand

We designed the graphics for four All Common drops: 65 pieces in all, from waffle crews and boxy fleece hoodies to heavyweight tees, camo long sleeves and sun-faded hoodies. Each one has a front and a back.

The challenge

All Common puts out its clothes in drops, and each drop needed new art that still looked like All Common. The brand also wanted to see every piece on the real garment, in the real color, before anything went to production.

The approach
  1. 01Drop 01 set the core: the wordmarks, the 88, the rabbit and the paisley, on waffle crews, fleece hoodies and camo tees.
  2. 02Drop 02 brought new artwork and added sleeve hits, so a piece can carry a second graphic down the arm.
  3. 03Drop 03 was 17 concepts with bigger illustrated backs: Alpha Aggression, Born Wild, King of the Jungle, Burning Earth and the first Our Father pieces.
  4. 04Drop 04 pushed further with chrome and rhinestone treatments, Legacy Reborn, and Our Father on tees, fleece and long sleeves.
  5. 05Built a lookbook for every drop that puts each graphic on the blank it will print on, front and back, with color swatches, so All Common could sign off on the real product.
