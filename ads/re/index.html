The challenge

Our real estate client needed to cut lead costs. CPL was sitting at $156.05.

The approach
  1. 01Swapped the image ads for video.
  2. 02Used clips that had already done well organically on Instagram, so we weren't guessing at what people would watch.
  3. 03Our bet: video would connect better with potential buyers and bring in leads more efficiently.
