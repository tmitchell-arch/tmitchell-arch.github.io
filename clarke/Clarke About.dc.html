ABOUT US

We teach children who are deaf or hard of hearing to listen and talk.

Every year, Clarke Schools for Hearing and Speech serves more than 1,000 children who are deaf or hard of hearing and their families.

Since 1867, preparing children for the wider world.

Clarke Schools for Hearing and Speech (formerly Clarke School for the Deaf) has prepared children who are deaf or hard of hearing to succeed in mainstream schools and beyond since our founding.

We also serve families and adults through a wide range of programs and services. With our extensive staff of experts, locations along the Eastern Seaboard and a robust Teleservices program, we reach people with hearing loss at all stages of life.
