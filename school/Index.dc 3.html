Four concepts

All three keep the current "Welcome to Saint Martin's / Lutheran School of Annapolis" header, then route visitors by audience: new families, current families, and church & friends.

Approved · Concept D v2

Homepage, with Oct 15 client edits

Concepts A–D below are kept as a parts library for the inner pages.
A · Funnel

Glass segmented control

Full-bleed photo hero. A frosted "I'm visiting as…" switch slides a spring pill between audiences, swaps the links, and crossfades the photo.
B · Doors

Three expanding panels

Centered editorial welcome on white. Below it, three photo doors; hover or tap one and it springs open with that audience's links.
C · Card stack

Swipeable audience cards

Navy hero with a deck of three cards. Flick the top card away (momentum carries it) or tap a path chip to bring a card forward.
D · Classic header

Template-style header + sentence funnel

Utility bar, dropdown nav, and "Schedule a visit," like the Erudatia template. The hero asks "I'm ___ and I want to ___" and sends people straight to the right page.
