About us

A Member-Owned Credit Union Built for Your Financial Growth.

None Suffer Lack Federal Credit Union (NSLFCU) is a member-owned credit union in Suitland, Maryland — here to help individuals and families build stability, opportunity and long-term growth.

Join NowMeet the Team
Who we serve

Built for Our Church Family — Nationwide.

Membership is open to members of From The Heart Church Ministries nationwide and their immediate family members.

Our purpose

A dependable place to save, borrow and manage your money with confidence — through responsible practices and service that puts members first.

01 Our mission

To improve our members’ financial quality of life with affordable products, responsible lending and outstanding service.

02 Our vision

To be a trusted financial partner for every member — with reliable services, modern digital banking and a lasting commitment to member success.
